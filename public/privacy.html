Privacy

Privacy notice.

How DW TEC uses personal information provided through enquiries, bookings and customer communications.

Who is responsible for your information?

Daniel Wilkinson, trading as DW TEC, is responsible for personal information handled by the business. For privacy enquiries, email info@dwtec.co.uk.

What information may be collected?

Depending on how you contact DW TEC and the work you request, this may include your name, contact details, vehicle make, model, year and registration, service location, details of the equipment or work required, photographs you send, messages relating to your enquiry, and booking, quotation, payment or invoice information.

Why is this information used?

Information is used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, arrange and carry out services, communicate about bookings, keep appropriate business records and deal with questions or issues relating to work carried out.

Depending on the purpose, the lawful basis may be taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, performing a contract with you, complying with a legal obligation, or the legitimate interests of operating and protecting the business.

Photos and vehicle information

Photographs and vehicle details sent for an assessment or quotation are used for that enquiry or service. Sending a photograph for a quote does not by itself give DW TEC permission to use that photograph for advertising or promotional purposes.

Facebook and Messenger

If you contact DW TEC through Facebook or Messenger, Meta also processes information through its services under its own privacy terms. DW TEC uses the information you provide there to respond to your enquiry, discuss a quotation, arrange work and communicate about the service you requested.

This website and cookies

DW TEC does not currently use website analytics, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. The website includes an enquiry form protected by Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent spam and abuse. Technical information such as IP addresses may be processed where necessary to deliver, secure and protect the website and form.

If non-essential cookies or tracking are added in future, this notice and any required consent controls will be updated before they are used.

Who information may be shared with

Personal information is not sold. It may be processed by service providers used to operate the business, such as email, website hosting or accounting services, where this is necessary. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

How long information is kept

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected. Records connected with completed work, payments, invoices or accounting may need to be retained for longer where legal, tax or record-keeping obligations apply.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights to ask for access to your personal information, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction, data portability or to object to certain uses. Not every right applies in every situation.

To make a privacy request, email info@dwtec.co.uk. You can also raise a concern with the Information Commissioner's Office if you are unhappy with how your information has been handled.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the way DW TEC handles information or the website changes.
