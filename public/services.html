DW TEC services

Mobile vehicle technology installations & windscreen chip repairs.

Every vehicle and installation is different. Prices shown are guide starting prices for labour only. Final labour is confirmed before booking once the vehicle, equipment and installation requirements are known. Any additional parts or installation accessories required are not included in the labour price and will need to be supplied separately. Where applicable, I can advise on suitable options and the parts required for installation.

Dashcam installation

Discreet installation and cable routing for customer-supplied dashcams. Wiring is routed securely along suitable factory routes, with trim removed or released where required and care taken around airbags, existing looms and moving sections such as tailgates. Installation time varies depending on the vehicle, power connection, fusebox location and the route required.

A suitable hardwire kit is required for customer-supplied dashcams. If needed, I can advise which kit is suitable for your vehicle and dashcam.
Front from £65
Front + rear from £110

Audio & stereo upgrades

Single-DIN and double-DIN stereo upgrades, DAB, Apple CarPlay/Android Auto, steering-wheel-control interfaces and selected speaker upgrades. Installation requirements vary significantly between vehicles depending on the fascia, interfaces, wiring, aerial equipment and any modification required.

Labour is quoted once the vehicle and required parts are confirmed. Straightforward installations may cost less than more involved fitting requiring interfaces, fascia changes, additional wiring or modification.
Single-DIN from £65
Double-DIN from £100

Reversing cameras

Aftermarket reversing-camera installation with discreet cable routing. Labour varies significantly depending on the vehicle, camera position, display compatibility, trim access and the route required from front to rear.

Labour is quoted once the vehicle and type of camera to be installed are confirmed.
